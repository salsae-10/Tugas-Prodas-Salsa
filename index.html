ABOUT ITOSHI SAE

Itoshi Sae is one of the most talented football players in Blue Lock and is the older brother of Itoshi Rin.

He is a highly skilled midfielder who plays with incredible accuracy, vision, and technique.

Sae is known for his calm, confident, and somewhat cold personality.

He once aimed to become the best striker in the world, but later chose to focus on being a midfielder. He is considered one of Japan’s greatest young football talents.
