THE PRODIGY

Since childhood, Itoshi Sae was recognized as an unmatchable football prodigy in Japan. Together with his younger brother, Itoshi Rin, they dominated youth leagues with ease.

Sae possessed natural instincts and technical skills that went far beyond his age group, inspiring Rin to follow in his footsteps.

However, after traveling to Europe to join Real Madrid's youth academy, Sae experienced the harsh reality of world-class football, which ultimately redefined his ambitions and football career forever.

GENIUS MIDFIELDER

Although Sae originally aimed to be the world's best striker, he shifted his path to become a playmaker—a genius midfielder who orchestrates the entire pitch.

His passing accuracy is mathematically precise, reading defenders' movements in advance and delivering razor-sharp passes that force strikers to perform at their absolute limits.

Sae values efficiency, logic, and calculated destruction over emotional gameplay, making him an unstoppable engine in midfield.

UNDERLASHES SENIOR

Characterized by his distinct long lower eyelashes and a sharp, composed gaze, Sae carries an aura of supreme confidence and intimidation.

As the older brother, his relationship with Rin transformed from loving mentorship into a cold, competitive rivalry after returning from Madrid.

Sae rarely shows emotions on or off the pitch, adopting a blunt and realistic attitude towards those who lack the talent or mindset to compete on the global stage.

JAPAN'S TREASURE

Sae is officially named one of the "New Generation World XI", making him a globally celebrated football prodigy and the pride of Japanese football.

Despite his disdain for the conservative nature of traditional Japanese football, his skills remain the benchmark for what Japanese athletes can achieve internationally.

His eventual participation in the Blue Lock vs. Japan U-20 match proved that he is not just a player, but a force capable of changing the future of Japanese sports forever.
